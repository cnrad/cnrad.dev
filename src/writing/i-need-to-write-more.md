---
title: I need to write more
date: August 16, 2026
---

I need to get better at writing.

Artificial intelligence is seemingly everywhere, and more people are outsourcing their thinking to LLMs — doing that is much easier than actually spending time thinking long and hard about something. But I can almost feel my thought process atrophying the more I do that, and I don’t want to fall into that trap. If anything, I’ll have more of an edge if I’m able to clearly articulate my ideas when others cannot.

When I write notes for myself or send messages to others, I convey what I need to just fine. But I don’t consider myself good at forming longer works/essays. I want to be able to express my thoughts clearly, and concisely, in a manner that’s easy to follow. Unfortunately, that means I need to do a *lot* of writing. 

There are many ways to “get good” at something, but ultimately 90% of it comes down to just *doing the thing*. This is not hard to do if you really enjoy doing the thing. I spent countless hours coding, drumming, or making electronic music as a kid simply because it was *fun* (and I’d consider myself quite good at those fields now). As one gets older, it usually becomes more difficult to devote a ton of time to something — especially if you don’t even want to do it in the first place.

Writing, to me, is not fun, therefore I avoid it. I have many ideas that make sense in my head, but when it comes to organizing them into a clear narrative, I get stuck. Because I want things I create to be perfect and they almost never are, I’ll often stay blocked and just give up. A lot of my work doesn’t see the light of day for this reason. However, there comes a point where I need to decide that good-enough is good-enough, and just hit “post” — this writing is an example of that.

I could write and write and share none of it, but if I’m not seeing anything through to the end, am I really getting better? Practicing only the first 70% of the process every time will make actually *completing* something more difficult. I would love to write something once and be done with it — but good writing forces you to think, and that’s the hardest part, and the part I want to become great at.

If you don’t know how to think, you can always read the thoughts of others. I try to consume good writing when I can, whether it be HN, blogs, or papers I find interesting enough to warrant reading. I have a great appreciation for clearly thought-out writing, and believe that consuming it will only help me in my journey. I should definitely start reading more books again, too. But I’ll never get any better if I only read — I need to write.
